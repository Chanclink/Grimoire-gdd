# The Word System

Words are the core mechanical tools of Grimoire. They function as both cards and weapons, bridging the gap between [[GRIMOIRE - Core Mechanics|action and turn-based combat]].

---

## What Are Words?

**Format**
- Single words or short phrases
- Example: "Leap", "Shatter", "Once Upon a Time"

**Identity**
- Mechanically expressive
- Thematic to [[GRIMOIRE - Genre System|genres]]

---

## Dual Functionality

### In Turn-Based Mode
Words function as **cards**:
- Deal Damage
- Afflicts debuff
- Buff yourself
- Manipulate your deck
- Shield/heal
- Have effect on the action mode
### In Action Mode
Words function as **weapons**:
- Top 3 cards become active "ammo"
- Each Word has limited duration/uses
- Expired Words move to discard
- Empty deck triggers reload vulnerability

---

## Shared Deck

> [!important] One Deck, Two Uses
> The same deck is used in both modes. This creates strategic tension:
> - Strong action cards may be weak in turn-based
> - Deck thinning affects both modes
> - Discard pile management matters everywhere

---

## Design Principles

**Mechanically Expressive**
- Each Word should have clear mechanical identity
- Visual + gameplay clarity

**Genre-Themed**
- Words fit [[GRIMOIRE - Genre System|genres]] 
- Comedy use Comedy-themed names (Laugh, Rehearsal comedy etc.)
- Tragedy use Tragedy-themed names (Pathos, Sorrow etc.)
- Other genres TBD (ideas include : Tales, Myth & Legends, Thriller, Polar, Fantasy)

**Synergy Potential**
- Words can combo together (potential "unexpected" combo between genre)
- Mode-switching synergies exist
- Build diversity matters
