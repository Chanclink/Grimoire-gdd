# Genre System

Each playable character embodies a **literary genre** but is not restricted to it, creating radically different playstyles while using the same core [[GRIMOIRE - Core Mechanics|dual combat system]].

---

## Launch Target

**4–5 genres** at launch

---

## Genre Structure

Each Genre has:

### Dedicated Character
- Unique character with thematic design
- Named protagonis
- While the dedicated character encourages building towards its genre, there is possibility to go for other genre

### Unique Starting Deck
- 8–10 [[GRIMOIRE - Word System|Words]] specific to the genre
- Words can be genre-exclusive or shared

### Unique Passive
- Core mechanical identity
- Always-on effect
- Defines playstyle lean

---

## Confirmed Genre: Comedy

### Character: Arlequin

**Archetype Theme**
Chaos and unpredictability

**Mechanical Identity**
- Random effects
- Crit chance
- Dodge rate
- Risk-reward design

**Playstyle**
High variance, high payoff when lucky

**Example Mechanics** (TBD specifics)
- Cards with random outcomes
- Bonus effects on crits
- Evasion-based survival
- Randomly changing stats

## Other Genres (TBD)

Design space to explore:

### Potential Genre Directions
- **Tragedy** - Resource drain, powerful finishers
- **Polar** - Information manipulation, card draw
- **Romance** - Synergy between cards, combos
- **Thriller** - DoT, debuffs, attrition
- **Sci-Fi** - Tech effects, transformation
- **Fantasy** - 4 Element, Fire, Ice, Electricity, Rock

> [!note] Design Requirements
> Genres must:
> - Play radically differently from each other
> - Mix well in hybrid builds
> - Be viable in both [[GRIMOIRE - Core Mechanics|action and turn-based modes]]
