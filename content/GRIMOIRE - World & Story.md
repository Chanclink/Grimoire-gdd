# World & Art Direction

## Visual Style

**Medium**
- 2D Pixel Art
- Top-down perspective in gameplay
- Dark fairytale aesthetic

**Inspirations**
- French tales (Perrault)
- Grimm Brothers
- Expanding into other literary traditions (TBD scope)

**Mood**
- Melancholic
- Slightly meta-narrative
- Abandonned world
- Impact of the non-existence of any sort of story in the people's mind

## Story Delivery

The narrative is fragmented and environmental, never interrupting gameplay flow.

### Delivery Methods
- **Snippets** - Brief text moments
- **Books** - Discoverable lore items
- **Environmental storytelling** - Visual narrative
- **Hub dialogue fragments** - Gradual reveals between runs

### Narrative Themes

**Core Concepts**
- Disappearance of narration
- Corruption of genres
- Collapse of stories
- The power of Words

# UI Design

Clean, readable, and thematically integrated.

---

## Action Mode UI

**Active Elements**
- Top 3 active [[GRIMOIRE - Word System|Word]] slots
- Reload indicator (progress/availability)
- HP display
- Ressource indicator (Mana/Action)
- Active relic indicators

**Design Goals**
- Minimal screen clutter
- Quick readability during action
- Clear feedback on card state

---

- Action points display

- Deck counter

- Enemy HP

**Design Goals**
- Clear tactical information

# Audio Direction

## Music

### Genre-Dependent Variations
- Each [[GRIMOIRE - Genre System|genre character]] has musical identity
- Themes reflect literary genre
- Dynamic layering system

### Combat Music
- **Action Mode** - Intense, rhythmic
- **Turn-Based Mode** - More contemplative, strategic
- **Dynamic shift** on mode transition
- Smooth crossfade or hard cut (TBD)

---

## Sound Design

### Literary Motifs
- Ink/quill sounds
- Page turning
- Book impacts
- Writing/erasing effects

### Fairytale Instrumentation
- Dark orchestral elements
- Storybook sound palette
- Corrupted/glitched versions for enemies

### Feedback
- Clear audio cues for:
  - Mode switching
  - Card expiration
  - Reload ready
  - Damage/hits
  - Boss phases
